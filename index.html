Update Mode

Put your Beyondex device in Update Mode

This page safely tells your device to restart into the built-in RP2040 update mode (BOOTSEL), so your computer can install new firmware.
1
Connect your device
Click Connect device. Your browser will show a device picker—choose “Beyondex HD Audio”.
2
Enter Update Mode
Click Enter Update Mode. The device will disconnect and reappear as the RP2040 BOOTSEL USB drive.
Download latest firmware (.uf2)
Not connected. Click “Connect device” to begin.
Device filter: VID=0xCAFE

What happens next?

  • Your device reboots into RP2040 BOOTSEL mode.
  • Your computer shows a new USB drive (the updater).
  • You copy the provided .uf2 firmware file to that drive.

Having trouble?

  • Use Chrome/Edge on desktop (WebUSB is not supported in Safari/Firefox).
  • If the device picker is empty, unplug/replug the device and try again.
  • If you changed USB IDs in firmware, update the VID in this page.
Privacy note: your browser will only allow access after you select a device in the picker. This page sends a single vendor request (bRequest=0x42, wValue=0xB007) and does not read audio data.
